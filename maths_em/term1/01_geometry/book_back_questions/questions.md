### Printed page 4 — Activity: drawing circles

- **Activity:** Draw circles using circular objects. Place a bangle or a coin on a paper. Trace its boundary. The picture you get is a circle. [Pictures: a bangle, two coins, a wheel, a CD]
- **Activity:** Let us draw the circle using a pencil and thread.
  1. Mark a point A on a sheet.
  2. Fix the thread at the point A and tie the pencil on the other end of the thread as shown in the figure.
  3. Move the pencil by keeping the thread at A. Point A is called the centre of the circle.
