### Printed page 69 — Clock hands and activity

- **Instruction (teaching):** Look at the clock. It has the numbers from 1 to 12 on its face. It has three hands. The shorter hand is the hour hand. The longer hand is the minute hand. The red colour hand is the second hand.
- **Teacher's note:** The teachers can make clock sheets without hands as per the number of students in the class.
- **Question:** Which hand moves faster?
- **Activity.** Students are given a blank clock and recording sheet. They draw hands on their clock (hour and minute) and leave it on their desk. Music begins and the students dance around, moving from desk to desk. When the music stops, students record whose desk they stopped at and the time on their clock.
