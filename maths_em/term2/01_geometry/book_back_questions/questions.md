### Printed page 4 — Paper folding activity and Exercise 1.1

- **Activity:** Let us understand the concept of symmetry by paper folding.
  1. Take a white sheet of paper and fold it once such that it is divided into two equal parts.
  2. Mark any two points say A and B on the folded line. As shown in figure.
  3. Draw a closed figure on the folded paper beginning at a point and ending at the other point.
  4. Cut the closed figure along the dotted lines marked.
  5. Unfold the paper. The half shapes on the either side of the line of fold AB are exactly the same.
  6. The line of the fold is the line of symmetry.
  - [Diagram sequence as printed: a rectangle, a folded rectangle with points A and B, a folded cut-out, the unfolded symmetric leaf-like figure with a dotted fold line]
